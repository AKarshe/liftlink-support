LiftLink

Garage control for Apple Home.

See every garage at a glance

LiftLink shows whether each garage door in your Apple Home is open, closed, or moving, and warns you when a door is obstructed, jammed, or can't be reached.

Protected by Face ID

Opening a garage always requires Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. You can require it for closing too.

Private by design

LiftLink talks only to Apple Home on your device. It has no account, no servers, no analytics, and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
